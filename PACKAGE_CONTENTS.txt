AMBATUDRONE PACKAGE CONTENTS
============================

droneControl4Motor.py
    Integrated PS4 bridge and dashboard. Shows percent, fixed motor direction,
    exact raw 1000-2000 us command, roll, pitch, relative yaw, and yaw rate.

INSTALL_AMBATUDRONE_WINDOWS.bat / BUILD_AMBATUDRONE_MAC.command
    One-click platform builder for AmbatuDrone.exe or AmbatuDrone.app.

RUN_SOURCE_WINDOWS.bat / RUN_SOURCE_MAC.command
    Runs the editable source after dependencies are installed.

Firmware/Primary_Teensy_Flight_Logger/
    Primary Teensy 4.1 combined flight controller and 48 kHz stereo logger.

Firmware/Feather_Ground_Radio/
    USB-to-915 MHz bridge for the computer-side Feather M0 RFM95.

Firmware/Feather_Airborne_Radio/
    915 MHz-to-UART bridge between airborne Feather and Teensy Serial1.

Firmware/Legacy_Separate_Boards/
    Previous direct ESP32 flight controller and independent Teensy logger.

Tools/extract_motor_run.py
    Converts RUN####.WAV + RUN####.JSON into audio WAV and current CSV.

START_HERE_*.txt
    Operating-system-specific installation and startup steps.

WIRING_AND_POWER.txt
    Full Teensy, Feather, Zeus, sensor, UART, and regulated-power wiring.

ESC_PWM_AND_LIMITS.txt
    Explains why 2000 us is the command maximum but not a motor-load guarantee.

ARDUINO_UPLOAD_GUIDE.txt
    Firmware upload sequence and required board/library support.

BENCH_TEST_CHECKLIST.txt
    Required propeller-free validation sequence.
