AMBATUDRONE TEENSY / FEATHER / ZEUS WIRING AND POWER
===================================================

REMOVE ALL PROPELLERS. VERIFY EVERY VOLTAGE WITH A MULTIMETER BEFORE PLUGGING
IT INTO A BOARD.


POWER ARCHITECTURE
------------------

The HGLRC Zeus 60A 4-in-1 ESC is rated for a 3S-6S LiPo but has NO BEC.
It does not provide a regulated 5 V output for the Teensy or Feather.

Use a separate regulated 5 V buck/BEC supply:

    6S battery + / -
        |-- Zeus ESC battery pads -> motors
        |
        `-- 6S-rated 5 V buck/BEC
                |-- regulated 5 V -> Teensy VIN
                |-- regulated 5 V -> airborne Feather through a dedicated
                |                    USB power cable/input
                `-- GND -> Teensy GND, Feather GND, and Zeus signal GND

Do not connect 22.2-25.2 V directly to Teensy VIN, Feather USB/BAT/3V, the
MPU-6050, microphone, or current-sensor signal pins.

Do not connect the externally powered Teensy or airborne Feather to a computer
USB port until USB/external-power backfeed has been handled correctly. The
simplest test rule is: disconnect the flight battery before plugging in USB.


TEENSY 4.1 CONNECTIONS
----------------------

Teensy pin        Connect to
----------        ----------
VIN               Regulated 5 V BEC output
GND               Common electronics/ESC signal ground
3.3V              MPU-6050 VCC, MAX4466 VCC, ACS72981 VCC
Pin 0 / RX1       Airborne Feather TX
Pin 1 / TX1       Airborne Feather RX
Pin 2             Momentary record button to GND (optional)
Pin 3             Zeus ESC motor signal 1
Pin 4             Zeus ESC motor signal 2
Pin 5             Zeus ESC motor signal 3
Pin 6             Zeus ESC motor signal 4
Pin 16 / A2       Filtered MAX4466 microphone output
Pin 17 / A3       Filtered ACS72981 current-sensor output
Pin 18 / SDA      MPU-6050 SDA
Pin 19 / SCL      MPU-6050 SCL
Built-in microSD  FAT32 microSD card

Never power a motor or ESC power lead from a Teensy pin.


AIRBORNE FEATHER UART
---------------------

Feather TX -> Teensy pin 0 / RX1
Feather RX <- Teensy pin 1 / TX1
Feather GND -> Teensy GND

Both UARTs use 3.3 V logic and 115200 baud. Keep these wires short and away
from the battery and motor-phase wires.


GROUND FEATHER
--------------

The ground Feather is powered by the computer's USB port. No UART wiring is
needed on the ground side. The Windows/Mac app selects its USB serial port.


SENSOR FILTERS
--------------

MAX4466 OUT -> 1 kOhm -> junction -> Teensy A2
                              |
                            10 nF
                              |
                             GND

ACS72981 OUT -> 1 kOhm -> junction -> Teensy A3
                                |
                              10 nF
                                |
                               GND

The 10 nF capacitor goes from the Teensy-side junction to GND. It is not in
series with the signal. Each sensor needs its own filter.


CURRENT SENSOR POWER PATH
-------------------------

Battery positive -> ACS72981 IP+ / current input
ACS72981 IP- / current output -> Zeus ESC positive battery pad
Battery negative -> Zeus ESC negative battery pad

Use the correct high-current wire, connectors, insulation, and strain relief.
The sensor's +/-50 A measurement range is below the Zeus ESC's possible total
four-motor current. It cannot measure or enforce the full 4 x 60 A ESC rating.


RADIO ANTENNAS
--------------

Attach the correct 900/915 MHz antenna to each RFM95 before transmitting.
Both sketches are set to 915.0 MHz and must match.
